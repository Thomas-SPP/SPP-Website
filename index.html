About SPP

The Saskatchewan Prosperity Project stands for provincial autonomy, resource sovereignty, and economic freedom. We believe Saskatchewan has been overlooked for too long, and it's time we took control of our own destiny. Through grassroots advocacy and community mobilization, we fight against federal overreach and promote policies that put Saskatchewan first.

Our Mission

  • Resource Sovereignty: Assert Saskatchewan’s right to develop and profit from our resources, free from punitive federal policies.
  • Provincial Autonomy: Defend Saskatchewan’s authority and push back against overreach that threatens our way of life.
  • Citizen Power: Build a strong coalition united in taking the future into our own hands.

Our Initiatives

  • Constitutional Rights: Advocacy for Saskatchewan’s control over resources and trade.
  • Energy Independence: Fighting to develop oil, gas, potash, and uranium sectors free from barriers.
  • Fiscal Fairness: Standing up for equitable treatment and keeping more of what we earn.
  • Community Mobilization: Organizing town halls, rallies, and campaigns for the province’s future.
