About SPP

The Saskatchewan Prosperity Project stands for provincial autonomy, resource sovereignty, and economic freedom. We believe Saskatchewan has been overlooked for too long, and it’s time we took control of our own destiny. Our province has the resources, the people, and the determination to chart our own course.

Through grassroots advocacy, policy research, and community mobilization, we fight against federal overreach and promote policies that put Saskatchewan first. From resource development to interprovincial trade, fiscal fairness to constitutional rights, we’re building a movement that refuses to be ignored.

Our Mission

  • Resource Sovereignty: Assert Saskatchewan's constitutional right to develop and profit from our own natural resources without federal interference.
  • Provincial Autonomy: Defend Saskatchewan’s jurisdictional authority and push back against federal overreach.
  • Citizen Power: Build a coalition of Saskatchewan citizens, businesses, and communities to take our future into our own hands.

Our Initiatives

  • Constitutional Rights: Researching and advocating for Saskatchewan’s authority over resources and trade.
  • Energy Independence: Fighting for the right to develop oil, gas, potash, and uranium sectors, free from federal controls.
  • Fiscal Fairness: Demanding equitable federal transfers and keeping more of what we earn.
  • Community Mobilization: Organizing town halls, rallies, and campaigns so Saskatchewan voices are heard nationwide.
